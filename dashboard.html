Now measured

The weekly re-run used to be unvalidated: every figure below came from a forecast made on 1 August. It has since been scored at six points in each of nine held-out seasons. Re-fitting does beat simply counting the league table — position RPS improves by 0.00214, better in 36 of 54 season-checkpoints, p < 0.0001 — and the gain peaks around 100 matches played, where it holds in nine seasons out of nine. It decays to nothing by 300. Late in a season the table, not the model, is doing the work.
